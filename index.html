Most analytics tools require you to hand over your Amazon credentials or grant third-party access to your KDP account.

KDP Tracker works differently. You download your monthly royalty report from Amazon and upload the numbers. Your raw files are parsed in your browser and never touch our servers.

Only the extracted numbers are stored, encrypted, in your private account. Your Excel files disappear the moment the numbers are read.
